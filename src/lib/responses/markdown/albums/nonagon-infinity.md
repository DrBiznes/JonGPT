# Nonagon Infinity

Released in 2016, Nonagon Infinity is a masterpiece of psychedelic rock that literally never ends - each track seamlessly transitions into the next, and the final track loops back to the first!

## Album Concept
The album is designed as an infinite loop, with each song flowing into the next through carefully crafted transitions. This creates a continuous listening experience that embodies the concept of infinity.

## Musical Style
- High-energy psychedelic rock
- Complex time signatures
- Recurring musical motifs
- Seamless track transitions

## Key Tracks
1. Robot Stop
2. Big Fig Wasp
3. Gamma Knife
4. People-Vultures

## Fun Facts
- The album can be started from any track and played in order to create a complete experience
- Many musical themes from this album appear in other KGLW records
- The word "nonagon" appears exactly 9 times in the album
